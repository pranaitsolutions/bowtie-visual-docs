# Privacy Policy — Bowtie Risk Visual for Power BI

**Effective:** September 2026 · **Publisher:** Prana IT Solutions Ltd

## Summary
The Bowtie Risk Visual processes your data entirely inside Power BI. It sends nothing anywhere.

## What the visual does with data
- Reads the columns you bind to its field wells from Power BI's DataView.
- Renders them on screen. All processing is in-memory, inside Power BI's sandboxed iframe.
- Makes **no network requests**. Its privileges declaration is empty.
- Uses **no cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage** or any other persistent client storage.
- Collects **no telemetry, analytics or usage data**.

## Data categories handled
Whatever you choose to bind: risk, cause, consequence, barrier and action identifiers, names,
statuses, dates, owners, health and effectiveness ratings, and any URL columns you supply for
hyperlinks. All of it comes from your Power BI model and stays there.

## Licensing
Premium licensing is verified through Microsoft's Power BI Licensing API. Prana IT Solutions Ltd
