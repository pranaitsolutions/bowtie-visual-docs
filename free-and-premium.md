## Getting a licence

Licences are sold and managed through Microsoft: buy from the
[Bowtie Risk Visual listing on AppSource](https://marketplace.microsoft.com/en-us/product/power-bi-visuals/prana-it-solutions.bowtie-risk-visual?tab=Overview),
then assign them to people in the [Microsoft 365 admin center](https://admin.microsoft.com/).
The buyer gets a licence automatically.

Licences come in two plans, **1–10 users** and **10–100 users**, and both have a **one-month free
trial**. For more than 100 users, ask us about volume pricing through a private plan.

A newly assigned licence can take up to an hour to be recognised. Then press **F5** in the Power BI
Service, or close and reopen Power BI Desktop.

## What people without a licence see

Every bowtie shows the free Grouped layout, whatever layout or perspective the report sets. The
report's settings are kept, so the same report shows the Premium views to licensed users.

While editing a report, Power BI shows a licence icon on the visual, and a short banner when the
visual is set to a Premium feature. Viewers in reading mode don't see the icon.

## Where licences can't be checked

Power BI can't check licences in some places, and the visual shows the free view there:

- Publish to Web and embedded reports
- Power BI Report Server
- National or regional clouds
- PDF and PowerPoint export through the REST API

In Power BI Desktop, sign in with the account the licence is assigned to. Signed out or offline,
Desktop can't check the licence and shows the free view.
